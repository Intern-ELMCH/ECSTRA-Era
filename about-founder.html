Visionary

Zaw Ali Khan is a director and entrepreneur from Lucknow, India, who is a prominent figure in the fields of healthcare and education technology. His career is defined by his passion for using science and technology to create positive social impact and improve people's quality of life. He is the Founder & CEO of Criterion Tech, the visionary behind ECSTRA at Era University and an Additional Director at Era's Lucknow Medical College and Hospital. With a strong focus on healthcare innovation, he has led the development of smart ICU systems, telemedicine platforms and simulation tools. He also leads True Form Games, creating immersive tools for medical education and holds multiple directorships in healthcare and education ventures.
